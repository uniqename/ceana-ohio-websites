Who We Are

About Columbus Ewe Habobor

Formed on August 25, 2002 by friends from the Volta Region of Ghana, Togo, and Benin, we exist to preserve culture, build community, and support development in Eweland.

🏛️

Our Mission

To preserve and promote Ewe cultural heritage, strengthen community bonds among Ewes in Central Ohio, and contribute to the social, educational, and economic advancement of Eweland.

🤝

What We Do

Organize social and educational activities, foster friendly relations in the greater Columbus area, and partner with CEANA on impactful projects in Ghana, Togo, and Benin.

🌍

Part of Something Bigger

As a proud member of the Council of Ewe Associations of North America (CEANA), we connect local action with continental impact across more than 20 associations.
