# CEANA Local Chapters – Shared Design System
## For Columbus Ewe Habobor & Ewe Association of Cincinnati-Dayton

### Brand Personality
Warm, vibrant, proud, community-centered, intergenerational, culturally rooted yet modern. Feels like a welcoming gathering place rather than a corporate nonprofit site.

### Color Palette (inspired by Ewe / Kente textiles)
- Primary Deep Red:     #8B1A1A  (Kente red – strength, heritage)
- Primary Gold:         #D4A017  (Kente gold – wealth, royalty, celebration)
- Primary Green:        #1B5E20  (Kente green – growth, land, hope)
- Deep Navy / Charcoal: #1A2332  (text, headers, grounding)
- Warm Cream / Off-white: #FDF8F0  (backgrounds – soft, inviting)
- Accent Teal:          #0D7377  (links, highlights, modern contrast)
- Soft Gold Tint:       #F5E6C8  (cards, section backgrounds)
- White:                #FFFFFF
- Text Dark:            #1A2332
- Text Muted:           #4A5568

Usage tips:
- Hero sections: deep red or navy with gold accents
- Buttons primary: gold background + dark text, or deep red
- Buttons secondary: outline in gold or teal
- Section backgrounds alternate cream ↔ soft gold tint ↔ white
- Never pure black; use deep navy/charcoal

### Typography
- Headings: "Playfair Display" or "Cormorant Garamond" (elegant serif – cultural gravitas)
  Fallback: Georgia, serif
- Body / UI: "Inter" or "Source Sans 3" (clean, highly readable)
  Fallback: system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- Accent / Ewe phrases: Playfair or a script if available

Google Fonts recommendation:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Playfair+Display:wght@500;600;700&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

### Layout & Spacing
- Max content width: 1140–1200px
- Generous padding: 4–6rem vertical on major sections (mobile: 3rem)
- Card radius: 12–16px
- Soft shadows: 0 4px 20px rgba(26,35,50,0.08)
- Consistent section rhythm

### Imagery Guidelines
- Hero: high-quality photos of people in traditional attire, community gatherings, or beautiful Ewe cultural elements (drums, kente, landscapes)
- Prefer authentic local chapter photos when available
- Use subtle overlays (dark gradient or color wash) for text readability
- Include a small “Proud Member of CEANA” badge in footer and about section

### Components
- Primary button: gold bg, dark text, rounded, hover slightly darker gold
- Secondary button: transparent with gold/teal border
- Cards: white or soft-gold bg, subtle border or shadow
- Navigation: sticky, clean, with logo left + menu right; mobile hamburger
- Footer: deep navy, multi-column with quick links, contact, social, CEANA affiliation

### Accessibility & Mobile
- High contrast text
- Focus states visible
- Touch targets ≥ 44px
- Semantic HTML
- Mobile-first responsive

### Voice & Tone
Welcoming, proud, inclusive, action-oriented. Mix English with occasional Ewe greetings/phrases (e.g., “Woezɔ” – Welcome, “Miawoe zɔ” – You are welcome). Celebrate both local Ohio community and connection to Eweland.
