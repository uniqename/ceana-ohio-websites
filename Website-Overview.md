# CEANA Ohio Chapter Websites – Overview Document

## Project Summary
Modern, engaging websites created for two local chapters of the Council of Ewe Associations of North America (CEANA):

1. **Columbus Ewe Habobor** (Central Ohio)
2. **Ewe Association of Cincinnati-Dayton**

These sites mirror the spirit and mission of the national CEANA website while feeling warmer, more local, and more community-focused.

---

## Design System
- **Colors**: Deep Kente red (#8B1A1A), Gold (#D4A017), Green (#1B5E20), Navy (#1A2332), Cream background
- **Fonts**: Playfair Display (headings) + Inter (body)
- **Style**: Clean, welcoming, culturally rooted, fully responsive

---

## Page Structure (both sites)
1. **Home** – Hero, welcome message, quick stats, mission cards
2. **About** – Local history, mission, connection to CEANA
3. **Culture & Heritage** – Traditions, community focus
4. **Events** – Upcoming gatherings + link to national convention
5. **Get Involved** – Membership interest, volunteer, donate
6. **Contact** – Form + leadership info
7. **Footer** – Navigation, CEANA affiliation badge

---

## Key Messages
- Columbus: Founded August 25, 2002. Preserving Ewe heritage in Central Ohio.
- Cincinnati-Dayton: Serving the Tri-State area. Registered 501(c)(3).
- Both: Proud member associations of CEANA supporting development in Eweland (Ghana, Togo, Benin).

## How to Launch
1. Open the HTML files to review.
2. Gather real photos, leadership names, and contact details.
3. Connect forms (Formspree recommended).
4. Choose a domain and free hosting (Netlify is easiest).
5. Go live and share with members.
