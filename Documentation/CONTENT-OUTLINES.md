## COLUMBUS EWE HABOBOR – Sample Copy

**Site Name / Branding:** Columbus Ewe Habobor  
**Tagline options:**  
- “Preserving Ewe Heritage in Central Ohio”  
- “Woezɔ – Welcome Home to Community”  
- “Building Bonds, Honoring Roots”

**Homepage Hero**  
Headline: Preserving Ewe Heritage. Building Community in Columbus.  
Subhead: Since 2002, Columbus Ewe Habobor has brought together Ewes and friends from Ghana, Togo, Benin and beyond to celebrate culture, support one another, and contribute to the development of Eweland.  
CTAs: Join Our Community | See Upcoming Events

**About – Our Story**  
The Columbus Ewe Habobor was formed on August 25, 2002 by a group of friends from the Volta Region of Ghana, Togo, and Benin living in Columbus and surrounding areas. What began as a gathering of friends has grown into a vibrant, culturally inspired association dedicated to maintaining and advancing Ewe heritage within the multicultural fabric of Ohio.  

We promote organized social and educational activities, foster friendly relations with all residents of the greater Columbus area, and work in partnership with the Council of Ewe Associations of North America (CEANA) to support meaningful development projects back home.

**Mission**  
To preserve and promote Ewe cultural heritage, strengthen community bonds among Ewes in Central Ohio, and contribute to the social, educational, and economic advancement of Eweland.

## EWE ASSOCIATION OF CINCINNATI-DAYTON – Sample Copy

**Site Name / Branding:** Ewe Association of Cincinnati-Dayton  
**Tagline options:**  
- “Unity, Culture & Community in the Tri-State”  
- “Celebrating Ewe Heritage Across Cincinnati & Dayton”  
- “Woezɔ – You Are Welcome”

**Homepage Hero**  
Headline: Celebrating Ewe Culture. Strengthening Community Across Cincinnati & Dayton.  
Subhead: The Ewe Association of Cincinnati-Dayton unites Ewes and friends in the greater Cincinnati and Dayton area. As a proud member of CEANA, we preserve our heritage, support one another, and invest in the future of Eweland.  
CTAs: Become a Member | Upcoming Gatherings | Support Our Work

**About – Our Story**  
The Ewe Association of Cincinnati-Dayton is a registered nonprofit dedicated to cultural preservation, community support, and connection to our ancestral homeland. We serve Ewes and friends across the Cincinnati–Dayton region, creating spaces for cultural celebration, mutual support, and meaningful contribution to development projects in Ghana, Togo, and Benin through our affiliation with the Council of Ewe Associations of North America (CEANA).

**Mission**  
To foster unity among Ewes in the Cincinnati-Dayton area, preserve and promote our rich cultural heritage, and partner with CEANA to support education, health, and community development in Eweland.
