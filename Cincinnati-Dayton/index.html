Who We Are

About the Ewe Association of Cincinnati-Dayton

We serve Ewes and friends across the Cincinnati–Dayton region, creating space for cultural celebration, mutual support, and contribution to development in Eweland.

🏛️

Our Mission

To foster unity among Ewes in the Cincinnati-Dayton area, preserve and promote our rich cultural heritage, and partner with CEANA to support education, health, and community development in Eweland.

🤝

What We Do

Host cultural and social gatherings, support members, engage youth and families, and channel collective energy toward CEANA’s development projects in Ghana, Togo, and Benin.

🌍

Part of CEANA

As a proud member association of the Council of Ewe Associations of North America, we connect local community life with a network of over 20 associations across North America.
